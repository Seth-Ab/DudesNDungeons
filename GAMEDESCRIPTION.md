## Vision

- A friendslop game where up to 4 friends can explore a randomly generated duengon together with proximity voicechat. 
- The map is very dark and the player can only see a small area arround themselves and other players, with map lighting being restricted to sparse torch pillars.
- players will explore to find items and unlock skills which help them to fight enemies, solve puzzles, and help them see greater distances.
- progression will be in a rougelite style where players keep items and skills between runs, and lose only items upon death. 
- the duengons will be generated from large room templates, these rooms can be large and sparce, maze like, have traps or puzzles that encourage cooperative play, and have enemies to fight
- in the duengons some of these rooms could have boss monters, and one final room will be for the survivors to escape back to a hub world.

## Core Pillars

- lighting mechanics 
- looting and fighting
- friendslop proximity chat and multiplayer interaction. 
- rougelite incremental progression

## Level Design

- levels are large in scale to allow players to lose eachother, maps should also have little to no light only having sparce light sources such as torches, firplaces, ect.
- players should not be able to see other players in different rooms and cant see or hear other players when they are within a short distance. 
- varying designs for rooms some could have mazes some could have traps, sometimes bosses, with loot and mosters randomly spread throught. 
- it should look like a duengon, with varried rooms with duengon themed decorations

## Movement

- the game is in a topdown style where the players charachter stays in the center of the screen, the map and other charachters move around them.
- players can move in any direction, players can not move through walls, objects, or other players. 

## Gameplay

- players join the game and start in a hub world with an entrance to the duengon, in the hub world players can get torches and store any items they pick up from the duengon. 
- once entering the duengon the map goes dark excluding any light sources from torches, monsters, or sparce map lighting.
- players will explore the duengon trying to get to the exit, if you stay in the duengon too long your resistance will start falling causing you to be progressivly more suceptable to damage.
- resistance is a general defensive stat. You can consume items or unlock skills to increase this value. The higher this number is, the less damage your character can take. 
- players have heath, resistance, and stamina bars aswell as 3 inventory slots to hold items in.
- stamina regenrates over time and is used when the player picks up objects or other players and durring combat.
